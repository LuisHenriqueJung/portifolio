Biometric PPE Delivery Platform

Workplace Safety · Production System · 1,000+ Users

Built a fullstack biometric verification system for fast PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) delivery in companies with hundreds of workers. Workers authenticate via face recognition or fingerprint, get matched to their equipment profile, and receive the right gear in seconds -- no manual ID checks, no paper logs. Includes a face-based employee search: point the camera at a worker and the system instantly identifies them and pulls up their full profile.
